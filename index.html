Our Story

The ice cream stand everyone remembers growing up.

Dairy Mart has been a fixture on Straits Highway since the mid-1950s. Walk-up windows, generous portions, and premium Michigan-made ice cream at fair prices. We serve Hudsonville hand-dipped from Holland alongside 30+ soft serve flavors that rotate all season. Plus burgers, dogs, and fries grilled to order.
